# Day 14-14: Cash Buyer Considerations

## Overview

Cash buyers face unique risks because they don't have a lender providing oversight. Two critical areas often missed are insurance requirements and the value of getting an appraisal despite not being required to do so.

---

## The Two Key Concerns

### What Gets Missed

> "There's two things where the homeless buyer could get into trouble. Number one, there's no bank telling them that they should get insurance. Number two... recommending an appraisal."

**With Financing:**
- Lender requires insurance
- Lender requires appraisal
- Lender verifies funds
- Lender reviews property

**With Cash:**
- No lender requirements
- Buyer must self-manage
- Easy to skip important steps

---

## Insurance for Cash Buyers

### Why It's Critical

**No Lender = No Mandate:**
- Lender always requires insurance
- Protects lender's collateral
- Cash buyer has no requirement

**What Could Happen:**
- Buy property uninsured
- Fire, flood, or disaster occurs
- Total financial loss
- No recovery possible

### Agent's Role

**Recommendation:**
- Remind cash buyers about insurance
- Not your job to verify, but should mention
- Connect with insurance providers
- Document the conversation

### Timing Considerations

**Before Closing:**
- Insurance should be bound
- Effective at closing
- Coverage immediately begins
- No gap in protection

---

## Appraisals for Cash Buyers

### The Common Assumption

**What Buyers Think:**
- "No lender = no appraisal needed"
- "I'm paying cash, I don't care about value"
- "Appraisal is just a lender requirement"

### Why Appraisals Still Matter

> "This is why an appraisal is important: so if you want to get one, you're not going to be able to negotiate the purchase price, but you should at least know what you're buying is worth."

**Value of Getting Appraisal:**
1. Know true property value
2. Documentation for future
3. Protection against overpaying
4. Reference for resale

### The Underwater Risk

> "If you're already paying more than what your house is actually worth and God forbid we have another market churn or something, you're automatically underwater."

**Without Appraisal:**
- No documentation of value
- May overpay significantly
- If market drops, trapped
- No record of purchase value

---

## Proof of Funds

### Why It's Essential

> "If somebody's coming to you saying 'I have $2 million to buy a house' and I'm supposed to rely on your word and find out 45 days later that you don't, we got to ask for proof of funds."

**What Can Happen Without:**
- Accept cash offer
- Remove financing contingency
- Buyer can't actually perform
- Deal falls apart late

### What Constitutes Proof

**Acceptable Documentation:**
- Bank statements
- Investment account statements
- Letter from financial institution
- Verification of liquid assets

**What It Should Show:**
- Sufficient funds for purchase
- Funds are liquid (accessible)
- In buyer's name
- Recent date

### Agent's Role

> "It's not necessarily your obligation as the agent to go as far as verifying the proof of funds, but you should at least be asking for them."

**Best Practice:**
- Request proof of funds with offer
- Include in contract package
- Let attorney verify details
- Don't proceed without it

---

## Cash Buyer Advantages

### For Sellers

**Why Sellers Love Cash:**
- No financing contingency
- Faster closing possible
- More certainty
- Fewer complications

### For Buyers

**Cash Offer Benefits:**
- More competitive offers
- No lender delays
- Simpler transaction
- More negotiating power

---

## Cash Buyer Risks

### Without Professional Oversight

**Lender Provides:**
- Appraisal requirement
- Insurance verification
- Title review
- Property evaluation

**Cash Buyer Must Self-Manage:**
- Get own appraisal (optional but smart)
- Arrange own insurance
- Rely on attorney for title
- Conduct own due diligence

### Overpaying Risk

**Emotional Purchase:**
- Fall in love with property
- Willing to pay anything
- Skip appraisal
- Later regret decision

---

## Advising Cash Buyers

### Recommended Approach

**Have the Conversation:**
1. "You don't need an appraisal, but I recommend one"
2. "Here's why knowing the value matters"
3. "You must get insurance before closing"
4. "Proof of funds will strengthen your offer"

### What to Explain

**Appraisal Benefits:**
- Know you're not overpaying
- Documentation for future sale
- Reference for insurance purposes
- Peace of mind

**Insurance Necessity:**
- Protect your investment
- Fire, flood, liability coverage
- Effective at closing
- Annual renewal

---

## Contract Considerations

### Cash Offer Provisions

**What Contract Should Include:**
- Cash purchase notation
- Proof of funds deadline
- No financing contingency
- Closing timeline

### Attorney Review Additions

**Typical Rider Items:**
- Proof of funds verification
- Insurance requirement before closing
- Clear title requirements
- Closing cost allocation

---

## Due Diligence Checklist for Cash Buyers

### Before Making Offer
- [ ] Verify available funds
- [ ] Obtain proof of funds documentation
- [ ] Consider getting appraisal
- [ ] Start insurance quotes

### During Contract Period
- [ ] Get inspections (still important!)
- [ ] Order title search
- [ ] Finalize insurance
- [ ] Optional: Order appraisal

### Before Closing
- [ ] Insurance bound and effective
- [ ] Funds ready to wire
- [ ] Title clear
- [ ] All due diligence complete

---

## Common Cash Buyer Mistakes

### Mistake 1: Skipping Inspections

**Why It Happens:** "I'm paying cash, I'll fix whatever"
**Why It's Wrong:** Major issues can cost more than expected

### Mistake 2: No Appraisal

**Why It Happens:** "Not required, why bother?"
**Why It's Wrong:** May significantly overpay

### Mistake 3: Delayed Insurance

**Why It Happens:** "I'll get it after closing"
**Why It's Wrong:** Property unprotected immediately

### Mistake 4: No Proof of Funds

**Why It Happens:** "I have the money, trust me"
**Why It's Wrong:** Wastes everyone's time if not true

---

## Key Takeaways

1. **Insurance Required:** No lender means buyer must self-manage
2. **Appraisal Valuable:** Know what you're buying is worth
3. **Proof of Funds Essential:** Include with cash offers
4. **Due Diligence Still Matters:** Inspections, title, etc.
5. **Agent Should Advise:** Have the conversation about risks
6. **Documentation Important:** Even without lender requirements
