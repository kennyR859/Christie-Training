# Day 14-13: Commission and Broker Fees

## Overview

Properly documenting commission and broker fees in the contract is essential. The MLS fee is frequently missed, and with recent changes to buyer agency, clear documentation of who pays what is more important than ever.

---

## The Importance of Complete Documentation

### What Gets Missed

> "A lot of the times I don't see the seller side commission filled out... everybody always misses the MLS fee."

**Common Omissions:**
- Seller side commission amount
- MLS fee
- Buyer side commission split
- Who pays what portion

### Consequences of Missing Information

> "I've actually had people not be paid the MLS fee when they were entitled to it because it just wasn't in the contract."

**What Can Happen:**
- Commission disputes
- Delayed payments
- Arbitration
- Lost fees

---

## What to Include in Contract

### Commission Section

**Fill In Completely:**
| Field | What to Include |
|-------|----------------|
| Listing Broker | Company name |
| Listing Agent | Your name |
| Commission % | Full amount |
| MLS Fee | Your actual MLS fee |
| Buyer Broker | Company name |
| Buyer Agent | Agent name |
| Commission % | Amount seller paying |

### MLS Fee Specifics

> "I don't know what your MLS fee is, so make sure you're putting in what your actual commission is due to you."

**Don't Assume:** Different MLSs have different fees.

---

## Attorney Review Rider Provisions

### What Tony Includes

> "I repeat everything in the rider. I put an indemnification in the rider from the other party to whoever I'm representing."

**Rider Additions:**
1. Commission amounts restated
2. Indemnification clause
3. Third-party claim protection
4. Buyer agency agreement reference

### Indemnification Clause

**Purpose:**
> "If there's a claim from third parties but also if there's a claim from the participating broker for more than what's listed in the contract."

**What It Protects Against:**
- Claims from other brokers
- Claims for additional commission
- Disputes about who represented whom

---

## Buyer Agency Agreement Conflicts

### The New Reality

> "You guys have your buyer agency agreements now, so you might have the buyer signed off at 3% but the seller's only required to give 2%."

**Potential Conflict:**
- Buyer agency agreement: 3%
- Seller paying: 2%
- Gap: 1%

### Protecting Against Claims

> "I don't want a buyer pursuing a buyer's agent now pursuing me as a seller for that extra 1%. That's not what we have in the contract."

**Solution:** Clear documentation in contract AND rider.

### When Attorney Requests Copy

> "If I see in the contract that the buyer's contributing something, then I'll ask for it."

**Tony's Practice:**
- Requests buyer agency agreement when buyer paying portion
- Verifies contract matches agreement
- Ensures consistency

---

## Documentation Trail

### Why Paper Trail Matters

> "The more you have to support you... the better your case is."

**Documents to Keep:**
1. Buyer's agency agreement (with date and percentage)
2. Contract commission section
3. Attorney review rider
4. Any amendments or modifications
5. Communication about changes

### Building Your Case

**If Dispute Arises:**
- "Here's the buyer's agency agreement - June 3%"
- "Here's the contract - sellers paying 2%, buyers paying 1%"
- "Here's the attorney review rider confirming"

## Arbitration Prevention

### Common Disputes

**Types of Commission Disputes:**
- Procuring cause arguments
- Commission percentage disagreements
- Who represented whom
- Dual agency confusion

### Prevention Through Documentation

> "You guys go to plenty of arbitrations and see disputes and stuff happens. And it matters what's in writing every step of the way."

**Best Practices:**
- Document everything in writing
- Get signatures on agreements
- Include in contract
- Repeat in rider
- Keep copies of everything

## Post-NAR Settlement Considerations

### Changes in Commission Structure

**New Reality:**
- Buyer agency agreements required
- Commission no longer automatic from MLS
- Negotiation between parties
- More documentation needed

### Protecting All Parties

**In Rider, Tony Addresses:**
- Seller not liable for amounts beyond contract
- Buyer responsible for their agreement
- No claims against other party
- Clear separation of obligations

## Filling Out Commission Section

### Step-by-Step

**1. Listing Side:**
```
Listing Broker: Christie's Real Estate
Commission: ___% of purchase price
MLS Fee: $___
```

**2. Buyer Side:**
```
Buyer Broker: ___________
Commission from Seller: ___%
Buyer Paying Additional: ___%
```

**3. Verify Totals:**
- Does seller side match listing agreement?
- Does buyer side match buyer agency agreement?
- Is MLS fee included?

## Common Scenarios

### Scenario 1: Standard Split

**Situation:**
- Listing agreement: 5% total
- Split: 2.5% each side
- MLS fee: $200

**Contract Shows:**
- Seller pays 5%
- 2.5% to listing broker + $200 MLS
- 2.5% to buyer broker

### Scenario 2: Buyer Agency Gap

**Situation:**
- Buyer agency agreement: 3%
- Seller offering: 2%
- Gap: 1%

**Contract Shows:**
- Seller pays 2% to buyer broker
- Note: Buyer responsible for additional per buyer agency agreement
- Rider clarifies buyer/buyer agent arrangement

### Scenario 3: No Buyer Broker

**Situation:**
- Unrepresented buyer
- Seller keeping full commission
- Listing agent only

**Contract Shows:**
- Commission to listing broker only
- No buyer broker involved
- Clear documentation of unrepresented status

---

## Rider Language Examples

### Commission Reiteration

> "The commission to be paid to the Listing Broker is X% of the purchase price plus $X MLS fee."

### Indemnification

> "Seller/Buyer represents they have not worked with any other broker, realtor, or agent. If any claim is made by any party for commission, the representing party shall indemnify and hold harmless the other party."

### Buyer Agency Clarification

> "Buyer's broker compensation shall not exceed the amount set forth in the contract. Any additional compensation owed to buyer's broker pursuant to a separate buyer agency agreement shall be the sole responsibility of the buyer."

## Best Practices

### For Listing Agents

1. Fill in ALL commission fields
2. Include MLS fee
3. Document in listing agreement first
4. Verify contract matches listing agreement
5. Review rider for accuracy

### For Buyer's Agents

1. Have signed buyer agency agreement
2. Know what seller is offering
3. Document any buyer contribution
4. Ensure contract reflects agreement
5. Provide copy to attorney if requested

### For Both Sides

1. Keep copies of everything
2. Document any changes in writing
3. Review rider carefully
4. Ask questions if unclear
5. Don't assume - verify

---

## Key Takeaways

1. **Fill Everything:** Commission section completely filled out

3. **Rider Repeats:** Attorney restates for clarity
4. **Indemnification Added:** Protection against claims
5. **Documentation Matters:** Build paper trail
6. **New Rules Apply:** Buyer agency agreements change dynamics
